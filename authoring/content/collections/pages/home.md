# Publish statically. Keep the discussion alive.

This site is authored in Statamic, generated by the official Statamic SSG package, and served without PHP. The same DiscussionBridge addon used by the live Flat and DB profiles prepares discussion identity before every build.

## Four modes, one generated site

- [**Simple comments** — A generated reply snapshot that refreshes with current public comments on page load.](/discussionbridge/simple-comments/)
- [**Full comments** — Discourse Core's plugin-free live comments frame.](/discussionbridge/full-comments/)
- [**Publishing through The Bridge** — A Statamic entry resolved into one durable Bridge Record and topic.](/discussionbridge/publishing-through-the-bridge/)
- [**From The Bridge** — Forum-owned content rendered safely during generation with live replies.](/discussionbridge/from-the-bridge/)

## What is—and is not—running here

The deployed site contains generated HTML only. The protected build application owns the receiver credential, delivery table, and generation command. Readers receive no secret, no Statamic control panel, and no hidden PHP application.
